# Business Problem

## 1. Business Context

Healthcare insurance organizations process large volumes of claims across members, providers, healthcare services, diagnoses, and geographic regions.

Business and operations teams need timely visibility into claims activity, healthcare spending, utilization, and provider performance to identify trends and support data-driven decisions.

However, claims and cost information can be distributed across multiple datasets, reports, and analytical workflows, making it difficult to maintain a consistent and centralized view of healthcare performance.

## 2. Problem Statement

Stakeholders need a centralized analytics solution that provides a consistent view of healthcare insurance claims, spending, allowed amounts, utilization, and provider performance.

Without a standardized analytics experience, teams may face:

* Fragmented claims and healthcare cost reporting
* Inconsistent KPI definitions
* Time-consuming manual analysis
* Limited visibility into provider-level performance
* Difficulty identifying healthcare spending trends
* Limited ability to drill from high-level KPIs into detailed claims data
* Slower decision-making due to disconnected reporting

## 3. Business Impact

These challenges can make it difficult for stakeholders to quickly answer important business questions such as:

* How is healthcare spending changing over time?
* Which services contribute most to total spend?
* Which providers have the highest claims activity?
* How does Allowed Amount compare with Claim Amount?
* Which regions have higher healthcare utilization?
* What factors are contributing to changes in claims cost?
* Are there unusual changes in claims volume or utilization?

## 4. Opportunity

A centralized healthcare insurance analytics dashboard can provide a single analytical experience for monitoring claims and healthcare cost performance.

The solution will combine standardized KPIs, interactive visualizations, provider-level analysis, claims drilldowns, and SQL-driven data validation.

## 5. Proposed Solution

The proposed **US Healthcare Insurance Analytics Dashboard** will provide:

* Executive-level claims and spending KPIs
* Claims trend analysis
* Allowed Amount and Claim Amount analysis
* Healthcare spend analysis
* Provider performance analytics
* Member and utilization analytics
* Geographic analysis
* Interactive filtering and drilldowns
* Standardized KPI definitions
* SQL-based analytical and validation queries

## 6. Portfolio Scope

This project is a portfolio-safe implementation using entirely synthetic data.

It demonstrates Business Analyst and Product Management capabilities across:

**Business Problem → Requirements → KPI Design → Data Modeling → SQL → Dashboard UX → Product Metrics → Architecture → Risk Management**

No real member, provider, claims, PHI, or proprietary organizational data is used.
